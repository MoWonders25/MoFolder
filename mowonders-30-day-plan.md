## Daily routine (30–45 min)
1. **Post** the day's Short on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (same file, platform-specific caption).
2. **Reply** to every comment within a few hours.
3. **Comment** genuinely on 5–10 videos in your niche (science, survival, "what if").
4. **Check** one stat: % viewed on yesterday's Short.

## Posting rules that matter most
- **Hook text on screen in the first second.** End with a question that invites comments.
- **Shorts: 20–45 s.** Loop-friendly endings get replays.
- **#Shorts** plus 3 niche hashtags. Label realistic AI footage as altered/synthetic.
- **Consistent time of day** (pick one evening slot, e.g. 6–8 pm your time).
